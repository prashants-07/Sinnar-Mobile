Your enquiry. Your information.

How Sinnar Mobile uses the information you share with the shop.

Information you provide

Contact and repair forms collect your name, phone number, optional email, message and, for repairs, your device and service details. We use this information to respond to your request.

Storage and access

Enquiries are saved in the shop’s database and are available to authorised administrators. Contact the shop to request a correction or deletion. The shop should delete enquiry records when they are no longer needed.

Third-party services

Opening WhatsApp, email or Google Maps uses those providers’ services. The embedded map may send information such as your IP address to Google. Their own privacy terms apply.

Cookies

The admin dashboard uses a session cookie to keep an administrator signed in and protect changes. The public website does not use advertising or analytics cookies.

Contact

For questions about your enquiry information, email sinnarmobile0@gmail.com or call 7620050500.
